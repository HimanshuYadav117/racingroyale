# Racing Royale terms and community rules


_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

Welcome to the grid! These terms are the deal between you and us.
"We" means IcanSolo Studios, the maker of Racing Royale.
By playing Racing Royale, you agree to these terms.
Please read them once. They are short, we promise.
Our [privacy policy](privacy.html) explains what data the game uses.

## The short version

- Race hard, trap hard, be kind.
- No cheating. No hate. No bullying.
- Keep your voice and nickname fit for a 13-year-old.
- Mute, report or kick anyone who spoils the fun.
- Break the rules and we may suspend or ban your account.

## 1. Who can play

- Racing Royale is for players aged 13 and over.
- Under 13? The game stays offline: you can race bots in practice.
- Under 18? Ask a parent or guardian before you play online.
- Players under 18 get a safe mode. The privacy policy explains it.

## 2. Your game account

- The game makes an account for your phone. No email or password needed.
- Anyone who has your phone can play as you. Keep it safe.
- You can delete your account at any time.
- Go to Settings > Privacy > Delete my account.

## 3. Community rules

These rules cover voice chat, nicknames, emotes, reports and races.

**Do not:**

- harass, bully, threaten or stalk anyone;
- use hate speech, or mock anyone for who they are;
- say anything sexual, and never anything sexual about a child;
- ask players for personal details, photos or to meet up;
- share anyone's personal details, your own included;
- swear at players or abuse them (friendly banter is fine);
- blast noise, music or loud sounds into the mic;
- pretend to be us, a moderator or another player;
- push scams, gambling, drugs, weapons or self-harm;
- pick a nickname that breaks any of these rules;
- cheat, hack, mod the game, use bots or abuse bugs;
- crash rooms, flood our servers or attack our services;
- sell, buy or trade accounts or coins for real money.

"Who they are" means things like race, caste, religion or nationality.
It also means gender, sexuality, disability and age.

**Do:**

- keep your traps sneaky and your voice friendly;
- mute or report anyone who breaks the rules;
- tell us about bugs in Settings > About > Report a problem.

## 4. Voice chat

- Voice chat is live. We never record it, so we cannot replay it.
- Voice is not encrypted. Talk as if you were in a public place.
- In public rooms you hold a button to talk, unless you pick open mic.
- To mute a player on your phone, tap their name in the lobby.
- You can switch voice chat off at any time in Settings > Voice.

## 5. Reports and what we do

- To report a player, tap their name in the lobby.
- After a race, you can also tap REPORT A PLAYER on the results screen.
- The room's host or leader can kick a player from the room.
- We review every report and act on it within 15 days.
- If someone breaks these rules, we may warn them.
- We may also suspend their account, or ban it for good.
- Think we got it wrong? Email us and we will look again.

## 6. Fair play

- Our room servers check that every move a car makes is possible.
- Moves that are not possible do not count.
- We keep a fair-play score for each seat while a race runs.
- Nobody is banned or loses coins automatically.
- A person looks at the facts before we act on a cheating report.

## 7. Coins and items

- You earn coins by racing, and spend them on paints and other items.
- Coins and items are for fun. They have no value in real money.
- You cannot cash them out or move them to another account.
- We give you the right to use them in the game. We still own them.
- We may change prices and rewards to keep the game fair.
- Coins and items are deleted with your account.
- This version of the game has no real-money purchases.

## 8. Our game

- Racing Royale, its art, sounds and code belong to us or our licensors.
- Some parts are used under open licences. See Settings > About.
- You may stream your races and share videos and screenshots.
- Do not copy, sell, rent or reverse-engineer the game.

## 9. The game can change

- We update tracks, traps, rules and items. Some things may go away.
- Online play needs our servers, and they can be down at times.
- We may end online play one day. We will try to warn you a month early.

- We are not responsible for what other players say or do.
- As far as the law allows, we are not liable for indirect losses.
- Nothing here takes away rights you have by law as a consumer.

## 11. Stopping

- You can stop playing at any time and delete your account.
- We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms.

## 12. Law and disputes
