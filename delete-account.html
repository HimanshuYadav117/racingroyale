Delete your Racing Royale account

Racing Royale is a mobile racing game by ICanSolo Studios. This page explains how to delete your game account and the data that goes with it.

Fastest: from inside the game

  1. Open Racing Royale and tap Settings.
  2. Open Privacy and tap Delete my account.
  3. Read what will be deleted, tap CONTINUE, then DELETE MY ACCOUNT.

Your account is deleted at once. The game then starts fresh on your phone. You need an internet connection for this.

Without the game: ask us by email

Email racingroyale.support@gmail.com with the subject "Racing Royale account deletion" and tell us:

  • your friend code (8 letters and numbers, under Settings > About), and
  • your deletion request code (12 letters and numbers, under Settings > Privacy), or, if you bought something and no longer have your phone, a Google Play order number of that purchase (it starts with "GPA." and is in your Google Play purchase emails).

A friend code alone is not enough: players share friend codes, so we need the deletion request code (only your phone shows it) or an order number to know the account is yours. We never ask for your name, phone number or documents. We acknowledge your email within 24 hours and delete the account within 15 days.

What is deleted

  • Your game account and the install ID that signs your phone in.
  • Your nickname, friend code, friends, friend requests and invites.
  • Your coins, items, coin history, match results and weekly leaderboard entries.
  • Reports you sent to us from the game.
  • When you delete in the game: everything the game keeps on your phone.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records (the product, the date and any refund) are pseudonymised: kept without your account, linked only to Google's order number, for 8 years because tax and refund law requires them, then deleted automatically.
  • Our nightly server backups may still contain the account for up to 7 days; they are then overwritten.
  • A report another player made about you stays until it is handled, and is deleted automatically 12 months after it was sent.
  • Voice chat is never recorded, so there is nothing to delete.
  • Data that Google keeps for ads or Google Play is covered by Google's own controls: myaccount.google.com.
