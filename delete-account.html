Delete your Racing Royale account

Racing Royale is a mobile racing game by IcanSolo Studios. This page shows how to delete your game account and its data. You do not need the game installed to ask.

Fastest: from inside the game

  1. Open Racing Royale and tap Settings.
  2. Open Privacy and tap Delete my account.
  3. Read what will be deleted and tap CONTINUE.
  4. Tap DELETE MY ACCOUNT.

Your account is deleted at once. The game then starts fresh. You need an internet connection for this.

Without the game: ask us by email

Email racingroyale.support@gmail.com. Use the subject "Racing Royale account deletion". Tell us these two things:

  1. Your friend code: 8 letters and numbers. It is under Settings > About.
  2. Your deletion request code: 12 letters and numbers. It is under Settings > Privacy.

Did you ever buy something in the game and lose your phone? Then a Google Play order number works instead of the second code. It starts with "GPA." and is in your Google Play receipt email.

A friend code alone is not enough. Players share friend codes. The deletion request code shows the account is yours. Only your phone shows it.

We never ask for your name, phone number or documents. We reply within 24 hours. We delete the account within 15 days.

No code and no phone? Then we cannot tell the account is yours. Nobody can sign in to that account again without your phone.

What is deleted

  • Your game account and the install ID that signs your phone in.
  • Your nickname, friend code, friends, friend requests and invites.
  • Your coins, items, coin history and match results.
  • Your weekly leaderboard entries.
  • Your age band (under 18, or 18 and over).
  • Reports you sent us from the game.
  • If you delete in the game: everything the game keeps on your phone.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Our nightly server backups may hold the account for up to 7 days. Then they are overwritten.
  • Server logs may hold your nickname or IP address. This only happens if you were kicked or your connection was refused. Logs are overwritten as they fill, normally within a few weeks.
  • A report another player made about you is kept until we handle it. It is deleted automatically 12 months after it was sent.
  • If you ever bought something, the purchase record is kept for 8 years. Tax and refund law needs it. It is kept without your account. Only Google's order number links to it. Then it is deleted.
  • Voice chat is never recorded, so there is nothing to delete.
