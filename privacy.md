# Racing Royale privacy policy


_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

This policy explains what data the mobile game **Racing Royale** ("the game") collects,
why, where it goes, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it.

**Who we are.** The game is made by IcanSolo Studios, an independent game developer in
India ("we", "us"). We decide how your data
is used (we are the "controller" under the GDPR and the "Data Fiduciary" under India's Digital
Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). Contact: racingroyale.support@gmail.com.

## The short version

- To play online, the game makes an **account for your phone** automatically. You do not
  give us an email, a phone number or your real name.
- Other players see your **nickname**, your car and your results. Friends you add see
  whether you are online.
- **Voice chat** goes live to the other players in your room. We **never record or store
  it**. Your microphone is off until you tap it, and in public rooms it is push-to-talk
  unless you choose otherwise.
- The game asks your **birth year and month once** and keeps only whether you are under 18.
  Players under 18 get a safe mode: push-to-talk in every room, no purchases, and ads that
  are not personalised.
- We keep your coins, items and match results on our server so you keep them.
- **Ads** (Google AdMob), **purchases** (Google Play) and **crash reports** (Sentry) use some
  data as described below.
- You can **delete your account** in the game (Settings > Privacy > Delete my account) or by
  asking us at https://himanshuyadav117.github.io/racingroyale/delete-account.html.

## 1. What we collect and why

| Data | What it is used for | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| **Install ID**: a random number the game makes when you first play (not your phone's hardware ID or advertising ID) | Signing your phone in to your game account | Performing our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| **Game account**: account ID, sign-in sessions | Keeping your coins, items and results | Contract |
| **Friend code** (8 letters and numbers) | Letting friends find and add you | Contract |
| **Nickname** you choose or the game makes | Showing who you are to other players and on weekly leaderboards | Contract |
| **Friends, friend requests, invites, online status** (menu, lobby or match, and the room code) | Friends and invites | Contract |
| **Age band**: under 18 or 18 and over (the birth year and month you type are not kept) | Safe mode for players under 18 | Legal obligation and legitimate interest (protecting young players) |
| **Match results**: place, points, wins, rounds, trap hits | Coins, weekly leaderboards, daily limits | Contract |
| **Coins, items and their history** | The in-game economy | Contract |
| **Purchases**: Google's order number, the product, purchase time, refund state (once purchases are switched on) | Giving you what you bought, restoring it, handling refunds | Contract; keeping records for tax law (Art. 6(1)(c)) |
| **IP address** | Connecting you to rooms; limiting abuse (too many requests, crashed rooms) | Legitimate interest in running a fair and secure service (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| **Voice**, while your microphone is on | Live voice chat with the players in your room | Contract (you switch it on) |
| **Anti-cheat scores** in server rooms | Spotting cheating | Legitimate interest (fair play) |
| **Reports** you send about a player (reason, match, room, the player's seat and nickname) or about a problem (your text and the app version) | Moderation and support | Legitimate interest; legal duty to handle complaints |
| **Server logs**: technical records of errors, refused connections (with the IP address) and kicked players (with the nickname) | Running and repairing the service, finding abuse | Legitimate interest (a working, secure service) |
| **Ad data** (once ads are switched on): advertising ID, app set ID, IP address (approximate location), ad views and taps, diagnostics | Showing and measuring ads, preventing ad fraud | Consent for personalised ads where the law requires it (Art. 6(1)(a)); legitimate interest otherwise |
| **Crash reports** (once switched on): what the game was doing when it failed, phone model, Android and app version, a random crash-report install ID | Fixing crashes | Legitimate interest (a working game) |

For Indian users, we process this data with your consent, given when you tap CONTINUE on the
game's first-run privacy notice, and for the legitimate uses the DPDP Act allows. You can
withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

**We do not collect:** your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location,
or payment card details (Google Play handles payments). We do not sell your data.

## 2. Who can see your data

- **Other players** in your rooms see your nickname, car, nameplate, results and emotes,
  and hear you when your microphone is on. Everyone can see the weekly leaderboards
  (nickname, score, nameplate). Friends see your online status.
- **When a phone hosts the room** (a local Wi-Fi game, or when our servers are full), that
  phone and our relay server see the players' IP addresses.
- **Google**: Google Play processes purchases; Google AdMob (once ads are on) collects the
  ad data above as an independent controller. See
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Sentry** (Functional Software, Inc.), once crash reports are on, processes crash
  reports for us as our service provider: https://sentry.io/privacy/
- **Oracle Cloud** hosts our servers for us (as our processor).
- We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

## 3. Where your data is processed

Our servers are in India. Google and Sentry may process data in the United States and
other countries under their own safeguards (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

## 4. How long we keep it

| Data | Kept |
|---|---|
| Account, friend code, nickname, friends, coins, items, match results, leaderboard entries | Until you delete your account |
| Online status, invites | Minutes (not saved) |
| Voice | Not kept at all |
| IP addresses | In memory for minutes; in server logs only for refused or abusive connections, removed as logs roll over (normally within days to a few weeks) |
| Your reports and problem reports | Until handled, and deleted automatically 12 months after you send them |
| Purchase records | Until you delete your account; then **pseudonymised** (kept without your account, linked only to Google's order number) for 8 years for tax and refund law, then deleted automatically |
| Server logs | Overwritten as they roll over (normally days to a few weeks) |
| Age band | Until you delete your account |
| Server backups | A deleted account can stay in our nightly backups for up to 7 days |
| Crash reports (Sentry) | Up to 90 days |
| Ad data | Google's retention: https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| Data on your phone | Until you delete your account, reset the game or uninstall it |

## 5. Your choices and rights

- **Delete your account:** Settings > Privacy > Delete my account. This deletes your account,
  coins, items, results, leaderboard entries, friend code, friends and reports you filed
  from our servers, and everything the game keeps on your phone. It cannot be undone.
  Without the game, use https://himanshuyadav117.github.io/racingroyale/delete-account.html: quote the deletion request code from
  Settings > Privacy (or a Google Play order number if you bought something and lost your
  phone). A friend code alone is not proof, because players share friend codes.
- **Microphone:** off until you tap it; mute any player by tapping their name in the lobby;
  switch voice chat off in Settings > Voice; revoke the permission in Android settings.
- **Ads:** Settings > Privacy > Privacy options (ads) changes your consent where it applies.
  You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
- **Your rights.** Depending on where you live you can ask us to access, correct, delete or
  export your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent. In India
  (DPDP Act) you can ask for a summary of your data and who it was shared with, its
  correction and erasure, nominate someone to act for you, and complain to us first and then
  to the Data Protection Board of India. In the EU and UK you can complain to your data
  protection authority. Write to racingroyale.support@gmail.com from the game's Settings > About >
  Report a problem or by email, and tell us your friend code (Settings > About) so we can
  find your account.

## 6. Grievances and contact

Grievance officer: the IcanSolo Studios privacy contact, racingroyale.support@gmail.com.
Complaints about the game, other players' behaviour or your data are **acknowledged at once**
(in the game, Settings > About > Report a problem shows a reference number straight away;
by email we acknowledge within 24 hours) and **resolved within 15 days**. You can also
report a player in the game (tap their name in the lobby, or REPORT A PLAYER on the results
screen).

## 7. Children and young players

Racing Royale is for players aged **13 and over** and is not directed at children under 13.
The first time you play, the game asks your birth year and month (it keeps only whether you
are under 18). **Players under 18 get a safe mode:** push-to-talk voice in every room, no
purchases ("Purchases need a parent's OK. Coming soon."), and ads that are not personalised.
This cannot be switched off later; only a new account (after deleting this one) asks again.
A way for a parent to give consent comes later. If you believe a child under 13 has an
account, write to us and we will delete it. `<OWNER: have a lawyer check this section and

## 8. Security

Sign-in, accounts, coins, purchases, leaderboards and friends use encrypted connections
(HTTPS). **Real-time game and voice traffic is not encrypted** (it uses UDP for speed): do not
say anything in voice chat that you would not say out loud in a public place. Our servers
are reachable only on the ports the game needs, secrets stay off the code, and the server
software is updated regularly.

## 9. Changes

We will update this page when the game changes and change the date at the top. For
important changes we will tell you in the game.
