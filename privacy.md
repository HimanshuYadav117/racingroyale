# Racing Royale privacy policy


_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

This policy explains what data the mobile game **Racing Royale** uses.
It says why we use it, who sees it, how long we keep it and your choices.

**Who we are.** Racing Royale is made by IcanSolo Studios.
IcanSolo Studios is an independent game developer in India.
Our legal name and country are shown on the game's Google Play page.
We decide how your data is used.
That makes us the "controller" under the GDPR.
Under India's DPDP Act, 2023, it makes us the "Data Fiduciary".
Privacy contact: racingroyale.support@gmail.com.

## The short version

- To play online, the game makes an **account for your phone** by itself.
- You never give us an email, a phone number or your real name.
- Other players see your **nickname**, your car and your results.
- Friends you add see whether you are online.
- **Voice chat** goes live to the players in your room.
- We **never record or store** voice chat.
- Your mic stays off until you tap it.
- In public rooms, voice is push-to-talk unless you choose open mic.
- The game asks your **birth year and month once**.
- It keeps only whether you are under 18, not the date itself.
- Players under 18 get a **safe mode** (see section 7).
- We keep your coins, items and match results on our server.
- This version of the game has **no ads, no purchases and no crash reports**.
- We **do not sell** your data. We do not share it for advertising.
- You can **delete your account** in the game at any time.
- Go to Settings > Privacy > Delete my account.
- Without the game, use our [account deletion page](delete-account.html).

## 1. What we collect and why

| Data | What we use it for | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| **Install ID**: a random number the game makes on first start. It is not your phone's hardware ID or advertising ID. | Signing your phone in to your game account | Contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| **Game account**: account ID and sign-in sessions | Keeping your coins, items and results | Contract |
| **Friend code** (8 letters and numbers) | Letting friends find and add you | Contract |
| **Nickname** you type, or one the game makes up | Showing who you are in rooms and on weekly leaderboards | Contract |
| **Friends, friend requests, invites and online status** | Friends and room invites. Status means menu, lobby or race, and the room code. | Contract |
| **Age band**: under 18, or 18 and over. The birth date you type is not kept. | Safe mode for players under 18 | Legal duty, and protecting young players (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f)) |
| **Match results**: place, points, wins, rounds, trap hits | Coins, weekly leaderboards, daily limits | Contract |
| **Coins, items and their history** | The in-game economy | Contract |
| **IP address** | Connecting you to rooms. Limiting abuse, like too many requests. | Running a fair and secure service (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| **Voice**, only while your mic is on | Live voice chat with the players in your room | Contract (you switch it on) |
| **Fair-play data** in our server rooms (see below) | Spotting cheating | Fair play (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| **Player reports**: the reason, match, room, seat and the player's nickname | Moderation | Fair play, and our duty to handle complaints |
| **Problem reports**: your text and the app version | Support and complaints | Our duty to handle complaints |
| **Server logs**: errors. Refused connections, with the IP address. Kicked players, by nickname. | Running and repairing the service, finding abuse | A working, secure service (Art. 6(1)(f)) |

**Fair play.** Our room servers check that each car's moves are possible.
Moves that are not possible do not count.
The server keeps a fair-play score for each seat while a race runs.
Each phone also tells the server what it saw of the other cars.
That is kept in memory for one race only.
If a score gets high, a server log notes the seat number and the score.
Nothing happens to you automatically: no ban and no lost coins.
A person looks at the facts before we act on a report.

**Abuse limits.** An address that keeps crashing rooms is refused for minutes.

**For players in India.** We use this data with your consent.
You give it when you tap CONTINUE on the game's first privacy screen.
Some uses are also allowed by the DPDP Act without consent.
You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

**We do not collect:** your real name, email, phone number or contacts.
We do not collect your photos, files, precise location or payment details.

## 2. Who can see your data

- **Other players** in your room see your nickname, car and nameplate.
- They also see your results and emotes.
- They hear you while your mic is on.
- **Everyone** can see the weekly leaderboards: nickname, score, nameplate.
- **Friends** see your online status and can invite you to rooms.
- **A phone that hosts a room** sees the IP addresses of the players in it.
- This happens in a Wi-Fi game, or when our servers are full.
- Our relay server also sees those addresses.
- **Oracle Cloud** hosts our servers for us, as our service provider.
- **Google Play** delivers the game to you under Google's own policy.
- We give data to authorities only when the law requires it.
- We do not sell data, and we do not share it for advertising.

## 3. Where your data is processed

Our servers are in India. Oracle Cloud runs them for us.
If you play from another country, your data travels to India.

## 4. How long we keep it

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| Account, friend code, nickname and friends. Coins, items, results, leaderboards. | Until you delete your account |
| Age band | Until you delete your account |
| Online status, invites | A few minutes, in memory only |
| Voice | Not kept at all |
| Fair-play scores and what phones saw | Memory: one race. Server logs: as below. |
| IP addresses | In memory for minutes. In server logs only for refused or abusive connections. |
| Your player reports and problem reports | Deleted automatically 12 months after you send them |
| Server logs | Overwritten as they fill up, normally within days to a few weeks |
| Server backups | A deleted account can stay in nightly backups for up to 7 days |
| Data on your phone | Until you delete your account, reset the game or uninstall it |

## 5. Your choices and rights

**Delete your account.** In the game: Settings > Privacy > Delete my account.
This deletes your account, coins, items, results and leaderboard entries.
It also deletes your friend code, friends and the reports you sent.
It wipes everything the game keeps on your phone.
It cannot be undone.

**Delete without the game.** Use our [deletion page](delete-account.html).
You need the deletion request code from Settings > Privacy.
A friend code alone is not proof, because players share friend codes.

**Microphone.** The mic is off until you tap it.
Tap a player's name in the lobby to mute them.
Switch voice chat off in Settings > Voice.
You can also remove the mic permission in Android settings.

**Your rights.** Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

- show you the data we hold about you, or send you a copy;
- correct it or delete it;
- stop or limit a use of it, or object to it;
- withdraw your consent.

In India (DPDP Act), you can ask for a summary of your data.
You can ask who we shared it with, and ask us to correct or erase it.
You can name someone to act for you if you die or cannot act.
You can complain to us first, then to the Data Protection Board of India.
In the EU and UK, you can complain to your data protection authority.

To use a right, write to racingroyale.support@gmail.com.
Or use Settings > About > Report a problem in the game.
Tell us your friend code (Settings > About) so we can find your account.
We answer within 15 days.

## 6. Complaints and contact

Grievance officer: the IcanSolo Studios privacy contact.
Email: racingroyale.support@gmail.com.
Complaints about the game, other players or your data are welcome.
In the game, Settings > About > Report a problem shows a reference at once.
By email, we confirm we got your message within 24 hours.
We resolve every complaint within 15 days.
To report a player, tap their name in the lobby.
After a race, you can also tap REPORT A PLAYER on the results screen.

## 7. Children and young players

Racing Royale is for players aged **13 and over**.
It is not made for children under 13.
The first time you play, the game asks your birth year and month.
It keeps only whether you are under 18.

**Players under 18 get a safe mode:**

- voice chat is push-to-talk in every room;
- the game blocks all real-money purchases;
- if ads are ever added, they will not be personalised.

Safe mode cannot be switched off later on the same account.
Only a new account asks for the birth date again.
India's DPDP Act treats everyone under 18 as a child.
We are building a way for a parent or guardian to give consent.
If you are a parent and want your child's account deleted, email us.
If a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

## 8. Security

Sign-in, accounts, coins, leaderboards and friends use HTTPS.
**Live game and voice traffic is not encrypted.** It uses UDP for speed.
So do not say anything in voice chat you would not say in public.
Our servers only open the ports the game needs.
Secrets stay out of the code, and we update the server software.
If a data breach puts you at risk, we will tell you without delay.
Where the law requires it, we will also tell the authorities.

## 9. Ads, purchases and crash reports

This version of Racing Royale shows **no ads**.
It has **no real-money purchases**.
It sends **no crash reports**.
If we add any of these, we will update this policy first.
We will also tell you in the game before the change applies.
